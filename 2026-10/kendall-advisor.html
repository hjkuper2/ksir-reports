The read

The year was built in the first half. Volume was up 40% at the end of August and is up 22.9% now. August, September and October each brought three closings, against 7, 11 and 7 a year ago.

The average and median disagree. Average up 15% to $1,111,013, median down 10% to $664,950. Ten sales at $2 million or more lift the average. Use the median with clients.

Softer terms, thin sample. Sale to list 96.3% to 93.5%, days on market 61.5 to 76, on only 36 and 35 listing side sales. The direction has now held since August.
