The read

Price did the work, not pace. Closings rose 3.7% while volume rose 16.6%. The average sale moved $180,000 higher. Growth here came from the top of the market, not from transaction count.

Sale to list improved while the firm held flat. 96.7% against 96.3% last year, on 292 listing side sales. Travis is the only large region where this metric moved up.

June was the peak. 88 closings and $132.6 million. August fell to 50 closings and $81.3 million, the softest month since January. Watch the autumn pipeline.
